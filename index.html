How it works

Three steps. Zero hassle.

  1. 01

    Stand in front of the mirror

    A quick scan reads your measurements. Stay fully dressed: no tape measure, no changing room.

  2. 02

    Drop your picks on the tray

    Put down everything you like. ScaNoa recognises every item instantly, with no scanning or barcodes.

  3. 03

    See what fits

    Watch it on your virtual self and get a clear answer for each item: fits well, runs tight or runs loose, plus which size to grab instead.

    Questions

    Good to know.

    Do shoppers need to change clothes to be scanned?

    No. They stay fully dressed. For the best result, bulky layers like jackets come off before the scan.

    Will it replace trying clothes on?

    It's a shortcut. ScaNoa helps shoppers pick the right size first, so anyone who still wants to try something on only takes in the one that fits.

    What happens to the body scan?

    No photos are stored. Measurements are used only for that session and deleted when it ends.
